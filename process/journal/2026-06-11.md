# Notes on touch interactions

We fine tuned a lot our interactions. We use a visual language of tap = zoom-in, zoom-out. Then when you hold press you get the full zoom. 

The cup if you fill it below the step 1 (33%), nothing happens when you release your finger. When you press again you can pass step 1 then if you release it's final. -> the zoom-out starts.

We spent a lot of time fine tuning the cup and bike long press, at first people didn't understand they had to long press on the screen.
